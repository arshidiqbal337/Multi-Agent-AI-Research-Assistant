Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines information retrieval with text generation to produce responses grounded in external knowledge.

A typical RAG workflow includes document collection, text processing, retrieval of relevant passages, and answer generation.

The retrieval component identifies information related to the user's question from a document collection.

Retrieved evidence can help reduce unsupported claims, but it does not guarantee that every generated answer is factually correct.

Source attribution allows users to inspect the documents and passages used to support a response.

The quality of a RAG system depends on document quality, retrieval relevance, and the reliability of the answer generation process.

The current Multi-Agent AI Research Assistant is an offline extractive prototype that uses keyword-based retrieval rather than a full language-model-powered RAG pipeline.
